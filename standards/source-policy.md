# Source and Data Policy

## Acceptable sources

- Peer-reviewed research
- Official product, platform, model, and dataset documentation
- Government, university, intergovernmental, and standards-body publications
- Authoritative technical reports
- Clearly identified practitioner sources when primary evidence is unavailable

## Required source metadata

- Title
- Author or organization
- Publication or release date
- URL or stable identifier
- Access date
- Source type
- Geographic and temporal scope
- License or permitted use
- Claim supported
- Known limitation

## Prohibited practices

- Inventing or reconstructing missing citations
- Citing a search result without reading the underlying source
- Using marketing material as proof of scientific performance
- Copying figures without license review and attribution
- Uploading private, restricted, Indigenous, community-governed, student, or partner data to unapproved services
- Treating model-generated references as verified

## Visual evidence

Generated imagery may illustrate a concept but must never be presented as observed environmental data, a real map, a measured result, or a documented event.
