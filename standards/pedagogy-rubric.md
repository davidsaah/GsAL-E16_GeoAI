# Pedagogy Rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4. A package must earn at least 30 of 36 points, receive no zero, and close every critical finding before proceeding.

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome alignment | Activities and assessment do not measure the outcomes | Partial alignment | Every outcome is taught, practiced, and assessed |
| Audience fit | Requires unprovided programming or GIS knowledge | Some assumptions remain | Accessible to the stated nonprogrammer audience |
| Conceptual progression | Fragmented or excessively abstract | Mostly logical | Concrete-to-abstract progression with clear transitions |
| Active learning | Passive lecture and procedural clicking | Limited reasoning tasks | Frequent prediction, interpretation, explanation, and verification |
| Spatial reasoning | Geography is incidental | Some spatial considerations | Scale, resolution, location, time, dependence, and transferability are explicit |
| Scaffolding | Students face unexplained leaps | Most steps supported | Model, guided practice, independent attempt, and reflection are balanced |
| Assessment quality | Rewards completion only | Some reasoning assessed | Measures interpretation, verification, method fit, and communication |
| Cognitive load and timing | Overloaded or cannot fit | Minor overload | Fits 55 lecture minutes and 100 planned lab minutes with buffers |
| Responsibility and inclusion | Ethics and accessibility absent | Treated as add-ons | Integrated into decisions, evidence, data use, and communication |

## Required pedagogical evidence

- Outcome-to-activity-to-assessment map
- Prior-knowledge statement
- Misconception inventory
- Time budget
- Lab checkpoints
- Verification task
- Accessible alternatives
- Instructor intervention points
