## Design rules

- Use Arial / Arial Bold and the documented USF palette: green `#00543C`, gold `#FDBB30`, gray `#919194`, with restrained supporting accents and contrast-safe body colors. Gold or gray on white is not a default body-text treatment.
- The current guide's lecture targets on its 13.333 by 7.5 inch, 16:9 canvas are titles 34-40 pt, body 24-28 pt and essential supporting labels at least 18 pt. These are course targets, not university-wide mandates or automatic accessibility certification. Preserve the existing canvas unless a change is assigned. Resolve the repository's older type targets explicitly in the conformance report; never resize a whole deck automatically.
- Never solve overflow by shrinking essential text or moving an essential limitation into tiny notes. Use a scoped layout repair, focused visual, authorized split or accessible equivalent; report changes affecting instruction to the assigned content writer.
- Use supplied original full-color GsAL artwork or the actual approved transparent PNG variants: black, white, USF green or USF gold in Compact, Long or Horizontal families. Preserve geometry, lettering, aspect ratio, alpha treatment and clear space. Do not redraw, manually recolor, stretch, rotate, add effects or invent a new lockup. Apply official asset rules to other USF marks.
- Use purposeful environmental imagery: Earth observations, terrain, oceans/coasts, wildlife/ecology, forests, deserts, field science and maps. Restrict photo-and-color mosaics to expressive openers/dividers where they help. Avoid generic corporate-AI decoration.
- Scientific meaning takes precedence over brand color: preserve scientifically appropriate map ramps, comparable legends/ranges/extents, units, missing-data categories, CRS/scale/time context and uncertainty. Never recolor or crop away evidence to match the brand.
- Keep observation, model output, human inference and synthetic illustration distinct. Never fabricate scientific observations, satellite scenes, maps or results. Label illustrative generated media and preserve consequential caveats next to their claims.
- Verify item-specific rights, credits and transformations for every external visual, including material from USGS, NOAA, NASA, USFWS, Copernicus or Natural Earth. A public URL, library listing or agency name is not blanket permission. Record source, asset ID/hash, date, reuse status, crop/adaptation and intended use. Flag uncertain assets exactly; do not invent clearance or replace them without an authorized grounded design process.
- Verify current faculty records, actual portrait identity and reuse status. Preserve requested source portraits; do not use archived biographies, invent portraits or perform unrequested generative retouching.
- Preserve useful alt text, logical reading order, captions, non-color cues, descriptive links, editable/selectable text and accessible map explanations. Report unavailable interaction, screen-reader, projection or classroom checks as NOT_RUN.
- Inspect final rendered changed pages and slide faces plus necessary surrounding context at readable scale; inspect the full assigned package for final design handoff. Check clipping, overlap, glyphs, contrast, logo clearance, credit legibility and link targets. Metadata or contact sheets alone do not establish visual or accessibility acceptance.
- Escalate scientific/instructional problems to their owner rather than hiding them through design. Do not change gates, approve your own work, publish or alter permissions.

## Handoff in every Producer cycle

Send the exact saved artifacts and the brand, attribution, accessibility and preservation reports to the Course Producer and Critic after each assigned design revision, not only after the last design pass. Report findings with artifact ID, slide/tab/element location, severity, governing source, proposed exact correction, owner and acceptance evidence. The Critic independently checks the applicable criteria and corrections against those versions; the Designer cannot approve its own output. This recurring handoff does not bypass workflow gates, formal Critic independence or final human review.
